ABOUT

From market insight to financial judgement.

My path into finance began with Marketing & Management, where I learned to examine customers, demand and how businesses create and communicate value. An MSc in Finance & Management then gave me the tools to connect those commercial drivers to financial performance, cash flow, risk and valuation.

Data and FinTech expanded how I structure evidence and test assumptions; project and stakeholder work taught me that analysis only matters when it is clear enough to support action. I now bring those perspectives together in business and investment analysis — understanding the business first, then using models and data to reach a defensible conclusion.

I am a Vietnamese finance graduate based in London. Outside finance, I enjoy volleyball, strength training, cooking and community projects.
